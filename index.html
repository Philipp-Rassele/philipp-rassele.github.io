About me

I hold a Master's degree in Computational Data Science and a Bachelor's in Computer Science and Engineering, both from the Free University of Bozen-Bolzano. My Master's thesis was about visual analytics for health and disease data in the CHRIS study. I built an interactive system that helps scientists explore large, complex biomedical datasets, with the analyst kept in the loop.

Since then I've worked on both the business and the engineering side. At the Raiffeisenverband Südtirol I supported the digitalisation programme and the business intelligence strategy. At Konverto I build ML models, MLOps pipelines with MLflow and CI/CD, Power BI reports and React/Vue front-ends.

I like to work on my own and in an organised way, and I enjoy making complex information easy to understand. I speak German natively, plus fluent English and good Italian.
